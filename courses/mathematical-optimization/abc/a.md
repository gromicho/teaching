# A — Alice

From a story to an objective; symbolic and numerical optimization. Suggested classes: 1.

## Learning progression

1. Derive the center-of-gravity objective and its feasible interval from the glass and water story.
2. Distinguish a symbolic stationary point, a feasible candidate, a local minimum and a global minimum.
3. Compare the same objective across symbolic mathematics and a numerical solver; explain the role of variable bounds.

## Discuss and investigate

- Why must endpoints be checked when optimizing over a closed interval?
- What changes when pi is a floating-point number instead of a symbol?
- Which dependencies are needed for symbolic work, plotting, model building and solving, respectively?

## Version choice

The 42-cell MO lecture retains the physical derivation, symbolic versus numeric pi, plots, Pyomo introspection and deliberate late solver installation. The current 10-cell public companion is tested but shorter; use it for execution, not as evidence that the longer narrative is already restored.
