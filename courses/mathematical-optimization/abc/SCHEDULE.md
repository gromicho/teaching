## Three capstone classes

19. Wind 1: turbine site selection, valid big-M bounds and strengthening. The maintained private key has real four-formulation comparisons on the original 110-site data.
20. Wind 2: continuous turbine placement, sea-depth costs and varying wind conditions. Prepare the selected historical source before execution.
21. Wind 3: derive a conic formulation, compare it with the convex formulation, and evaluate robust placements. The original Part 1 and Part 2 belong to one capstone, not two unrelated cases.

## Five practice sessions

- After F: Week 1 exercises on LP, integer formulations and big-M.
- After G: Week 2 exercises on convex/QP/conic models.
- After H: Week 3 exercises on Lagrangian relaxation and subgradients.
- After I: Week 4 exercises on farmer, newsvendor and airline stochastic models.
- After J/K: Week 5 exercises on robust farmer, portfolio and airline models.

The source solution packs are private. Student versions need the relevant question statements and should not contain grading notes or answer cells. The later wind and practice sources have been selected and preserved, not newly certified for execution.
