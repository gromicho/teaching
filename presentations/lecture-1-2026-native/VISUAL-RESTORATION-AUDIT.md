PowerPoint (`references/Lecture 1a - 2026.pptx`) remains the visual reference;
its individual embedded assets were preserved with recognisable names in

## Rights and maintenance rule

The `assets/visuals/lecture-1/manifest.json` file records original slide and
object provenance. `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` is the visual source ledger. A linked
source shows where students can inspect current information; it does not grant
reuse rights for inherited pictures. Before public release, replace or clear
all entries marked `needs-rights-review`.
