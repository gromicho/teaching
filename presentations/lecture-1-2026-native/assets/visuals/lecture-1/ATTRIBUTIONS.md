# Attribution ledger for restored Lecture 1 visuals

`manifest.json` records the original PPTX slide and object for every file in
this directory. It is the authoritative file-level ledger. `assets/source`
remains the unmodified extraction archive.

The following are verified or recommended teaching attributions for the
important visual families. They identify a source or a replacement candidate;
they do **not** turn an inherited PowerPoint picture into openly licensed
artwork.

| Visual family | Attribution or source to show | Reuse status |
|---|---|---|
| Gartner analytics model | Gartner, *Data and Analytics* framework; link to `https://www.gartner.com/en/topics/data-and-analytics` | Credit the framework. The embedded Gartner graphic itself remains a third-party teaching figure. |
| COVID prescriptive case | Bertsimas et al., “From predictions to prescriptions: A data-driven response to COVID-19”, *Health Care Management Science* (2020), `https://doi.org/10.1007/s10729-020-09542-0` | Cite the paper and retain the historical scenario date. |
| IoT device trend | IoT Analytics, “State of IoT 2025”, `https://iot-analytics.com/number-connected-iot-devices/` | Prefer the current native redraw; do not present the old forecast as an observation. |
| Storage and Moore’s-law charts | Our World in Data: `https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/historical-cost-of-computer-memory-and-storage` and `https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/transistors-per-microprocessor` | Prefer the linked data visualisations or a course-owned redraw. |
| TSP milestones | University of Waterloo TSP project: `https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/` and `https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/star/star100m.html` | Separate proven optimal tours from the 100-million-star demonstration. |
| Sustainable Development Goals | United Nations communications material: `https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/` | Use official artwork under its stated terms. |
| Dike-height case | Eijgenraam et al., *Operations Research* (2012), `https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1110.1028` | Cite the study; the inherited photographs need separate image-rights records. |
| Hunger / WFP case | World Food Programme supply-chain context: `https://www.wfp.org/supply-chain` | Individual inherited photographs still need a photographer and reuse-rights record. |
| LHC image / claim | CERN/ATLAS primary material: `https://atlas-public.web.cern.ch/updates/briefing/run-3-trigger` | Replace the inherited image if an explicit CERN licence is required. |

## Items deliberately marked `needs-rights-review`

The original slides contain no complete source line for these figures. Keep
them only inside the course archive until a source and licence are recorded:
the Four Vs infographic, Internet-in-one-minute graphic, Walmart image and
claim, several COVID graphics, the deep-learning infographic, and the older
dike, WFP and Timor-Leste picture collages. The existing visual review explains
replacement candidates where available.
