Construction rule
  Slides are assembled from native Beamer/TikZ text, shapes, tables, and
  individual PowerPoint picture/diagram objects. Complete-slide screenshots
  are not used as slide content. PowerPoint slide exports in the build folder
  exist only for visual QA and are not referenced by the TeX source.

Reusable Beamer construction
  abwlecture.cls defines the shared slide canvas, UvA/ABS master mark, title,
  footer, and semantic frame types for standard content, people, questions,
  answers, figures, tables, sections, and title slides. The compiled deck has
  75 pages. The six introduction slides are maintained as readable source in
  slides/people rather than as flattened slide images. Learning goals,
  instructional diagrams, the IoT update, solver evidence, Python and
  CodeGrade guidance, the driving/AF447 sequence, recaps, and the final call
  to action are maintained as native sources in slides/learning.

Existing embedded artwork
  assets/source contains individual graphic objects exported from the source
  PowerPoint. Existing visible source lines and attributions were retained.
  Inclusion here preserves the source deck's course context; it does not by
  itself establish broader republication rights for third-party artwork.

Named visual library and attribution ledger
  assets/visuals/lecture-1 contains 101 descriptively named copies of the
  embedded image objects from Lecture 1a - 2026.pptx. Each entry in
  assets/visuals/lecture-1/manifest.json records its original slide and object,
  its inherited source line when present, and whether rights still need review.
  ATTRIBUTIONS.md records the verified/recommended teaching citations and the
  visual families that must not be treated as cleared for broader reuse.
  assets/source remains unchanged as the opaque, forensic extraction archive.

Build
  From the pre-repository Lecture 1 workspace:
    py -B build\lecture1-beamer-native-20260901\generate_native_beamer.py
  Then from beamer\lecture-1-2026-native, compile
    xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error lecture-1-2026-native.tex
  twice.
